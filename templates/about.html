The proposition

Ability Bazaar is a global, accessibility-first marketplace where persons with disabilities and mixed creator teams use AI App Builder Agents to translate lived-experience problems into practical AI mini-apps. Each tool is delivered through a common accessible shell, validated by PwD users, and made available to individuals, institutions and employers. Vision-Aid's SEP AI Tutor serves as the anchor offering; the long-term scale engine is a trusted creator economy.

New platform, proven foundation

Ability Bazaar came online in July 2026, but it did not start from zero. Vision-Aid brings more than twenty years of listening to, learning from and delivering programs with persons with visual disabilities. That experience—and a broad network of learners, graduates, accessibility professionals, volunteers, educators, institutions and partners—gives Ability Bazaar the reach and practical knowledge to grow responsibly and quickly.

It rolls in DATTC

Our Digital Accessibility Testing and Training Center has sold accessibility testing for years - real customers, real revenue, real PwD professionals. Ability Bazaar becomes its storefront, and DATTC becomes the bazaar's proven services engine.

It rolls in the AI Development Center

Our newest initiative has already shipped the SEP AI Tutor and built Arohi and Nidra. Ability Bazaar becomes the front end to distribute and monetize these apps - and every app that follows.

Accountable, by design

A marketplace is judged on proof, not promises. Ability Bazaar captures evidence from day one - orders delivered, revenue earned, wages paid to experts with disabilities, and accessibility issues fixed.

Honest framing

We publish what we measure. Ability Bazaar is worth building in its own right: it aligns with what Vision-Aid already does, and extends it - turning lived experience into livelihood.

Nonprofit ownership

A Vision-Aid program on its own domain

Ability Bazaar and abilitybazaar.com are owned and operated by Vision-Aid, Inc., a United States 501(c)(3) public charity. The separate domain gives this disability-led technology program a clear, accessible home; it is not a separate company or unrelated commercial business.

Ability Bazaar directly advances Vision-Aid's charitable mission. It combines training, accessible technology and livelihood creation so people with disabilities can participate as learners, creators, accessibility experts and paid professionals. Revenue from its mission-related services supports delivery, training and paid work within that program.

Legal operator and contact: Vision-Aid, Inc., 8 Vine Brook Road, Lexington, Massachusetts 02421, United States · info@visionaid.org · (781) 333-5252.

Visit Vision-Aid's nonprofit website, where Ability Bazaar is also announced as a Vision-Aid initiative.

Open for business

A real marketplace, today

Ability Bazaar is open for mission-related work as a program operated by Vision-Aid, a 501(c)(3) public charity with more than two decades of experience working with persons with visual disabilities. The platform can immediately leverage Vision-Aid's established network while every delivered order creates skilled work for experts with disabilities.

Backed by supporters, too

Mission-related earned revenue helps Vision-Aid sustain the platform and create training and livelihood opportunities. Beginning in January 2027, organisations can support one Ability Fellow through a $1,000 training sponsorship covering accessible learning, mentoring, practical work, paid internship experience, and employment support. It is an invoiced workforce-development engagement with defined deliverables, not a donation. Explore the Ability Fellowship sponsorship, or if the mission simply inspires you, make a gift of any size.
