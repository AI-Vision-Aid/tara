Accessible documents

Make your PDFs, Word files and presentations accessible.

We remediate files against relevant WCAG 2.2 success criteria and, for PDFs, applicable PDF/UA criteria. Every file is verified with a real screen reader by a blind or low-vision professional before it comes back to you. Every order funds paid, skilled work for professionals with disabilities.

Planning for a public-sector deadline? The U.S. Department of Justice extended the ADA Title II web and mobile accessibility deadlines to April 26, 2027 for covered state and local public entities with populations of 50,000 or more, and April 26, 2028 for smaller entities and special district governments. Documents can be covered, subject to the rule's defined exceptions. Read our source-backed plain-language guide. This is general information, not legal advice.
