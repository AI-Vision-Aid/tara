Ability Bazaar offers automated accessibility scans, fixed-price website and app launch packages, and separately quoted custom services. If the site has fewer suitable pages, we review all available pages. The service is delivered within three business days after we receive a reachable URL and key-page choices; it is not a comprehensive manual audit or assistive-technology test. Accessible Document Remediation covers PDF, Word and PowerPoint files. Document Starter is $199 for up to 30 standard pages with delivery within 5 business days; Document Standard is $499 for up to 90 standard pages within 7 business days; and Document Pro is $899 for up to 180 standard pages within 10 business days.
